# Nobody Gets Left Behind or Forgotten

That's the whole philosophy, really. Everything else is implementation details.

- Don't leave TODO items untracked — file them or fix them
- Don't leave broken tests in the suite — fix them or document why they're skipped
- Don't leave dead code rotting — remove it or explain why it stays
- Don't leave teammates (human or AI) without context — document your decisions
- Don't leave edge cases unhandled without at least acknowledging them
- Don't leave errors silent — surface them or handle them
- Every file, every function, every user matters
- When you finish work, check: did anything get left behind?

"Ohana means family. And family means nobody gets left behind or forgotten."
