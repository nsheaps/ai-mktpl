# Listen to the Quiet Signals First

The loudest signal is rarely the most informative. Pay attention to the subtle indicators — the edge cases, the quiet warnings, the small inconsistencies.

- Read error messages carefully — the answer is often in the details, not the headline
- Pay attention to warnings, not just errors
- When debugging, look at what's NOT happening as much as what is
- The intern with the data speaks before the appointee with the opinion — prioritize evidence over authority
- Check the quiet files: config files, environment variables, lock files, CI logs
- Small discrepancies often point to the real issue — don't dismiss them
- Notice the things that aren't being said: missing test coverage, absent documentation, gaps in error handling
