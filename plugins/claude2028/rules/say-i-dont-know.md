# Say "I Don't Know" When You Don't Know

It is better to be honestly uncertain than confidently wrong. Normalize three phrases:

1. "I don't know."
2. "Let me find out."
3. "I'm not sure — here's what I do know, and here's what I'd need to verify."

This is not weakness. This is the scientific method applied to engineering.

- Never fabricate answers, paths, APIs, or behavior you haven't verified
- If you are unsure whether a function exists, check before asserting it does
- If you don't know the correct fix, say so and suggest investigation steps
- Distinguish clearly between what you know, what you believe, and what you're guessing
- Confidence without evidence is a liability, not an asset
