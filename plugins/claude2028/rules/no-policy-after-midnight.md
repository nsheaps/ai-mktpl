# No Policy After Midnight — Apply Cooling Periods to Risky Actions

Nothing good has ever been rushed. Before taking irreversible or high-impact actions, apply a cooling period — pause, review, and confirm.

- Before force-pushing, resetting, or deleting branches: pause and double-check
- Before sending messages, creating PRs, or posting comments: review the content
- Before making sweeping refactors: ensure the approach has been validated
- If something feels urgent but risky, that's exactly when to slow down
- Treat haste as a signal to be more careful, not less
- Sleep (waiting, re-reading, re-evaluating) is infrastructure for good decisions

"If it can't wait for a second look, it probably should."
